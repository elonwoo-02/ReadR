# Uszkoreit, Jakob

## Definition / Summary

Proposed replacing RNNs with self-attention and started the effort to evaluate this idea, which led to the Transformer. Also a co-author of the Vision Transformer (ViT) paper.

## Related Research

- Affiliation: Google Research → Google DeepMind
- Key papers: [[Attention Is All You Need (NeurIPS 2017)]], [[An Image is Worth 16x16 Words (ICLR 2021)]]

## Personal Notes

Jakob's initial proposal to replace RNNs with self-attention kicked off the entire Transformer line of research. His involvement in both the original Transformer and ViT shows the cross-modal impact of the idea.
