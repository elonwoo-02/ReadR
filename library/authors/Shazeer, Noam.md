# Shazeer, Noam

## Definition / Summary

Proposed scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention, and the parameter-free position representation in the Transformer paper. Also known for pioneering work on Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) at scale (GShard, Switch Transformer).

## Related Research

- Affiliation: Google Brain → Google DeepMind
- Key papers: [[Attention Is All You Need (NeurIPS 2017)]]

## Personal Notes

Noam proposed two of the most critical components of the Transformer (scaled dot-product attention and multi-head attention). His work on MoE has also been highly influential in scaling LLMs.
