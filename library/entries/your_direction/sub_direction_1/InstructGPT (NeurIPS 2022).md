# InstructGPT (aligning LLMs with human intent)

Fine-tunes GPT-3 with human feedback using reinforcement learning, showing that alignment techniques improve truthfulness and reduce harmful outputs while maintaining capabilities.

**Keywords:** RLHF, instruction tuning, alignment, human feedback
