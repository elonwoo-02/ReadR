# RAG (retrieval-augmented generation for knowledge tasks)

Combines a pre-trained seq2seq model with a differentiable dense retriever, enabling the model to access external knowledge without parameter updates.

**Keywords:** retrieval-augmented, generation, knowledge-intensive, dense retrieval
