# BERT (bidirectional encoder pre-training)

Pre-trains a deep bidirectional Transformer on masked language modeling and next-sentence prediction tasks, achieving state-of-the-art results on 11 NLP benchmarks.

**Keywords:** pre-training, bidirectional, transformer, language model
