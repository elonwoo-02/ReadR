# Transformer (foundational seq2seq architecture)

Introduces the Transformer architecture, which replaces recurrent layers with multi-head self-attention, achieving state-of-the-art translation quality with less training time.

**Keywords:** attention, transformer, sequence-to-sequence, machine translation
