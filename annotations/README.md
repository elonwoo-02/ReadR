# Annotations

This directory holds the **close-reading notes** produced during the
CLOSE-READ phase. One folder per paper, mirroring the `library/entries/`
sub-direction structure. For example:

```text
annotations/nlp/knowledge-graph/kg-augmented-llm/GFM-RAG (NeurIPS 2025)/reading-note.md
```

creates a note that mirrors the entry at
`library/entries/nlp/knowledge-graph/kg-augmented-llm/GFM-RAG (NeurIPS 2025).md`.

A reading note is the output of close reading — distinct from a paper entry
(the catalog record) and from a survey (the REVIEW phase).

---

## Structure

Each close-reading note lives in its own folder:

```text
<paper-name>/
├── reading-note.md      # the close-reading note (uses the template)
└── attachments/         # figures, tables, diagrams embedded in the note
```

The note is created from `_template/reading-note.md`.

## AI / Human division of labor (pre-gate)

Close-reading notes use a **pre-gate** (hard block) rather than a label,
because the highest-value judgments in a note must be human-authored.

- **AI (skeleton):** fills the section skeleton, formulas, and
  figure/table **placeholders** following the paper's actual structure.
- **Human (content):** fills all actual content — every formula, figure,
  table, and example must be *explained*; plus the entire
  "Personal Evaluation & Reflection" section.

Figures → `![Figure X: caption](attachments/fig-XX.png)` + interpretation.
Tables → rewritten as Markdown tables or cited as the original table + analysis.
Formulas → symbols, derivation logic, and purpose explained.

## `reading-note.md`

### Frontmatter

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `type` | Always `"Reading Note"`. |
| `paper-entry` | Wiki-link to the corresponding paper entry, e.g. `"[[GFM-RAG (NeurIPS 2025)]]"`. Required: a `close-read` entry points here. |
| `generated` / `verified` | Trust signals. A freshly drafted note is `generated: human` / `verified: unverified` and upgraded after review. |
| `created` / `updated` | Lifecycle dates. |

### Body

**Title:** `# Paper Title (Venue Year)` — the same title used in the
entry, self-contained for standalone sharing.

**Identity block** (blockquote, for standalone sharing):

```markdown
> **Title:** Paper Title
> **Venue:** Conference / Journal (Year)
> **Link:** URL
```

An `AI / Human Division of Labor` block follows, documenting who produced
what in this note.

**Overview paragraph:** one paragraph covering the paper's problem, method,
and key findings — no separate heading.

Then the main sections, generated by AI following the paper's own structure:

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `Research Background & Motivation` | Why the paper was done; the core problem. |
| `Core Method` | The method, fully explained — every component, formula, figure, and example. Internal sub-structure is filled by AI to match the actual paper (architecture diagram, key components, formula explanation, concrete example, etc.). |
| `Experiments & Results` | Experimental setup; then explain **each** figure and table — what it shows and what it means. |
| `Ablation / Parameter Analysis` | What each ablation reveals (sibling of Experiments). |

Finally the **pre-gated** human section:

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `Personal Evaluation & Reflection` | Your own judgment — personal understanding, inspiration, questions, and improvements. AI provides only guiding questions; the content is human-authored. |

> The skeleton is driven by the paper's structure, not by a fixed template
> subsection hierarchy. The template defines *what must be present*; the
> AI fills *how it is organized* to match each specific paper.
